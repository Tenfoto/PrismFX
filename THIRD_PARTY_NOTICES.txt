The standalone Windows distribution (`PrismFX-*-Windows-x64.zip`) is built in
PyInstaller `onedir` (directory) mode rather than `--onefile` static packing.
All Qt 6 and PySide6/Shiboken6 shared libraries (`Qt6Core.dll`, `Qt6Gui.dll`,
`Qt6Widgets.dll`, `Qt6OpenGL.dll`, `Qt6OpenGLWidgets.dll`, `shiboken6*.dll`,
`PySide6/*.pyd`, and Qt platform plugins) are preserved as separate dynamically
loaded shared libraries under `_internal/PySide6/` and `_internal/shiboken6/`.

Users may replace these shared library files in `_internal/PySide6/` and
`_internal/shiboken6/` with their own interface-compatible modified builds of
Qt 6, PySide6, or Shiboken6 compiled from the Corresponding Source above, and
run `PrismFX.exe` directly against the modified libraries without recompiling
PrismFX Studio. (Unused Qt modules such as QtWebEngine, QtPdf, QtMultimedia,
QtQuick3D, QtShaderTools, QtDesigner, QtSql, QtTest, QtQml, and
QtVirtualKeyboard are intentionally omitted from the standalone distribution.)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PrismFX Studio does NOT bundle or redistribute the `FC Minimal` font or any
third-party proprietary font files in this repository or in any release

automatically falls back to standard Windows system fonts (`Leelawadee UI`,
